Discord connection

Directly reading all channels from your logged-in Discord account is not enabled. Discord does not provide unrestricted account-history access through ordinary OAuth. This tool processes exports you already have permission to obtain; it does not scrape Discord sessions or extract tokens.

Supported input formats

JSON: array of message objects or DiscordChatExporter-style {messages:[...]}, including author, timestamp, content, attachments, and replies. CSV: header row with content/message, author, channel and date columns. TXT: plain text or lines such as [date] author: message.
